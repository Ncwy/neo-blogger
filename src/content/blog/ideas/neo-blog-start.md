# Neo Blog Start

## Why I build this blog

This blog is not only a place to publish articles.

It is a long-term personal knowledge system.

I want to use it to record:

- What I learn
- What I build
- What I think
- What I discover through experiments

## Three directions

### Trading

Record:

- Trading journal
- Strategy experiments
- Market observations
- Lessons learned

### Life

Record:

- Career Lab
- Learning system
- Personal growth
- Life experiments

### Ideas

Record:

- Thoughts
- Technology
- Business
- Systems

## The goal

The goal is not to write more.

The goal is to think better, build better systems, and accumulate knowledge over time.
